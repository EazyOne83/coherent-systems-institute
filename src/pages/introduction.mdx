## What CCF Is

The Coherent Consciousness Framework (CCF) is a **dynamical systems model of consciousness**.  
It treats consciousness as a **structured information process** that evolves over time, shaped by the interaction of distinct functional modes.

CCF does not define consciousness as:

- a substance  
- a location in the brain  
- a static property of matter  

Instead, it defines consciousness as the **behavior of an information system** moving through different modes of operation, each with its own stability profile, functional role, and contribution to experience.

---

## The Three Informational Modes

CCF identifies three fundamental modes that govern conscious dynamics:

### **Physical Mode of Consciousness (PMC)**  
Grounding, embodiment, sensory coupling, environmental modeling.

### **Entropic Mode of Consciousness (EMC)**  
Dissolution, instability, exploration, creative entropy, breakdown of rigid patterns.

### **Integrative Mode of Consciousness (IMC)**  
Synthesis, coherence, global meaning, consolidation of insight.

These modes are not “states of mind” in the everyday sense.  
They are **functional attractors** — stable patterns of information flow that the system can enter, exit, and transition between.

---

## Consciousness as Movement Through Modal Space

In CCF, conscious experience is defined by **movement**:

- how the system enters a mode  
- how it stabilizes  
- how it dissolves  
- how it transitions  
- how coherence emerges  

Consciousness is not any single mode.  
It is the **trajectory** the system takes as it moves through them.

This movement is shaped by:

- internal dynamics  
- environmental input  
- developmental history  
- noise and instability  
- integrative capacity  

The system’s ability to transition — especially through **productive instability** — determines whether coherent experience becomes possible.

---

## The Coherent Mode of Consciousness (CMC)

A coherent conscious state arises when the system enters the **Coherent Mode of Consciousness (CMC)** — a stable, integrative configuration characterized by:

- strong cross‑modal coupling  
- high integrative flow  
- reduced fragmentation  
- increased global meaning  
- structural coherence  

CMC is not a mystical state or a metaphor.  
It is a **formal attractor** representing the system’s highest level of integrative functioning.

---

## Why This Matters

Most existing theories explain only one dimension of consciousness:

- **IIT** explains integration  
- **GWT** explains access  
- **Predictive Processing** explains modeling  
- **Entropic Brain** explains dissolution  
- **Orch‑OR** speculates on substrate  

CCF provides the **missing architecture**:  
a unified model describing how different experiential modes arise, dissolve, and transition — and how coherence becomes possible.
