## Mission

The Coherent Consciousness Research Institute (CCRI) is dedicated to developing, formalizing, and validating the **Coherent Consciousness Framework (CCF)** — a unified model describing the structure, dynamics, and measurable properties of conscious systems.

Our work spans theoretical development, computational modeling, applied decision‑systems engineering, and cross‑disciplinary scientific integration.
